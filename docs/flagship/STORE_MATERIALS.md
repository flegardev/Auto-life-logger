# Store material draft — Auto Life Logger

## Short positioning

Understand your browsing. Keep your history private.

## Description

Spoznajte, katere spletne strani beležijo vaš čas brskanja, in si ustvarite mirnejši pregled svojih navad.

• Oglejte si zabeleženi čas, strani in zgodovino
• Preglejte sedemdnevni povzetek ter vzorce
• Sami določite, katere domene so za vas produktivne ali moteče
• Nastavite osebni dnevni cilj
• Po želji vklopite opozorila in druge ukrepe
• Izvozite, obnovite ali izbrišite lokalne podatke

Dejavnost se beleži v tem brskalniku in se ne pošilja strežniku. Povzetek zajema brskanje, ne celotne uporabe računalnika. Oznake domen so vaše nastavitve in ne merijo kakovosti dela.

## Update notes

Version 2.3.0 introduces the redesigned interface and workflows described above. Existing product identity and permission scope are unchanged.

## Support

Report a reproducible problem at https://github.com/flegardev/Auto-life-logger/issues. Include Chrome version, steps and redacted screenshots. Never attach browsing-history exports or private vehicle notes to a public issue.

## Screenshots

Use actual `docs/flagship/screenshots/` captures. Test data must be labeled illustrative; screenshots are not evidence of real personal activity or current vehicle availability. Existing icons remain in the package to preserve recognition.
